    About this dashboard

    Skyline pulls live forecast data straight from Open-Meteo's public weather API — no sign-up, no tracking. Search any city worldwide, or tap "My location" to see conditions where you are right now. Beyond the forecast, Skyline also shows sunrise/sunset times, the current moon phase, and real-time air quality (US AQI) sourced from CAMS atmospheric data. Toggle between °C and °F any time; every number on the page updates instantly.
